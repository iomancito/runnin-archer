Running Archer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Running Archer is a free game for Android with no ads and no in-app purchases. This policy explains what happens with your data when you play it.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or usage data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking services.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (gold, upgrades, unlocked levels, achievements) and your settings (volume, graphics quality) are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data from Android settings deletes it permanently.

Permissions

The game does not request any Android permissions and does not use the internet.

Children

Because the game collects no data at all, it does not collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any question about this policy, open an issue at github.com/iomancito/runnin-archer/issues.
